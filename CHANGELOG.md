## [1.1.0]

- Past weeks take pictures from the gallery: every week on the door frame has a gallery button, and folded runs of empty weeks open with a tap. What is picked goes into that week even when its own date is lost or elsewhere.

## [1.0.0]

- First release: a door frame for each child with a notch for every week of life that has a photo or a clip, shot with the phone's own camera or taken in from the gallery into the week it was taken.
- Smart reminders when a week has no picture, at the hour the family usually takes pictures; birthdays, half-birthdays and the month's postcard.
- Tree rings of a childhood, heights on the door frame, "on this day" and siblings at the same age.
- The same spot: an earlier picture as a ghost over the camera, and a film where the faces stay put and the child grows.
- A second a day, made into month and year films with the date and age in the corner.
- It will soon be gone: 42 things of each age to keep before they go.
- Their words, recorded in their own voice; the birthday interview on video; hands traced on A4 to scale.
- Backup to one zip. English and Russian.
